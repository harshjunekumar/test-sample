### Post 2: Churn business case
> 💡 **Contacting every customer at risk of churning would *lose* money. Targeting only the riskiest 10% returns 89%.**
>
> In my latest portfolio project, a subscription business was losing 18.8% of customers, about $1.09M in annual revenue.
>
> I followed the Descriptive → Diagnostic → Predictive → Prescriptive framework:
> 1️⃣ Churn was highest among customers with 5+ support tickets (45%) and those in their first 6 months (39%)
> 2️⃣ A logistic regression (AUC 0.81) confirmed the drivers: month-to-month contracts, support friction and skipped onboarding
> 3️⃣ I gave every active customer a risk score
> 4️⃣ I built the business case: a blanket campaign returns –47% ROI; a targeted one returns **+89%**
>
> The lesson: analysis matters when it changes *where the money goes*.
>
> Case study 👉 <repo-link>
>
> #BusinessAnalyst #CustomerRetention #Churn #DataDriven #Analytics
