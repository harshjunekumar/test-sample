# How to complete the case study

Files:
- `Savoury_Snacks_Analysis_Workbook.xlsx` – analysis engine (formulas only, no data yet)
- `PepsiCo_Savoury_Case_Study_Deck.pptx` – 10 slides + appendix; storyline, layouts and charts ready

## Step 1 – Load the data (Excel)
1. Open the case-study data file. Copy the **Sales Data** tab into the workbook's `Sales Data` sheet from A2.
   Re-order columns first so they match row 1: Week, Customer, Channel, Store Format, Supplier, Brand,
   Segment, Sub-segment, Pack Format, Value (£), Units.
2. `Inputs` sheet: set the PepsiCo supplier name (exact spelling), Year 1 / Year 2 week ranges,
   the focus customer and the online channel label.

## Step 2 – Run each analysis (type the names into the yellow cells)
| Tab | What to type | What you get | Deck slide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1_Segment | Segment names | Growth, contribution to growth, PepsiCo share, price index | 4, 5 |
| 2_Brand | Brand names | Brand growth & share, PepsiCo flag | 5 |
| 3_Whitespace | Sub-segments (rows) and pack formats (row 5) | Fair Share Index heatmap + £ opportunity | 6 |
| 4_Channel | Channel names + segments | Online vs total, online segment index | 7 |
| 5_Customer | Retailer names | PepsiCo share by retailer | 9 |
| 6_Acadia_Whitespace | Segments, sub-segments, packs | Same analysis for Acadia Grocers only | 9 |
| 7_Demographics | Paste Demographic Data | Online index by life stage / age / mission | 5, 7 |
| 8_Basket | Paste Basket_bands + CSN_metrics | Basket band % by channel, online vs offline | 7 |
| 9_CrossBuy | Paste Basket_Combination_Data | Affinity index + suggested deal type | 8 |
| 10_Prioritisation | Your actions + scores | Ranked recommendations | 10 |

Check row under each table should be **0**. If it isn't, a segment/brand name is missing or misspelt.

## Step 3 – Update the deck
1. On each chart: right-click > **Edit Data** and paste your numbers from the matching tab.
2. Replace every `[bracket]` and `xx` with your real finding. Make each title the **insight** (what the chart proves).
3. Delete the red **SAMPLE DATA** tags once a slide has your real numbers.
4. Write the Executive Summary (slide 2) **last**.
5. Speaker notes on each slide show the source tab and the talk track.

## Step 4 – Prepare for questions
- Why these priorities? → size × growth × gap × ease (tab 10 scores)
- Data limits? → no buyer-level panel, sanitised data, 2-year window
- How to measure success? → share in target cells, AWOP, new buyers, online conversion
