Recommendations

Biggest leverFix how negative interactions end52% of contacts are negative or very negative, and they average 2.5–4.5 CSAT. Train agents in de-escalation and empathy, add a supervisor call-back for very-negative contacts, and track the sentiment change from start to end of each contact.
DemandRemove the reasons people call71% of contacts are billing questions. Clearer invoices, refund and payment status in the app, and a self-serve billing FAQ reduce contact volume and the frustration that comes with it.
FeedbackClose the survey blind spotOnly 37% of contacts get a CSAT score. Use a one-tap in-app survey sent right after the contact, and link CSAT to repeat-purchase data so retention can be measured directly.
Don't over-investKeep SLA and AHT as hygiene metricsResponse time and call length show no link to CSAT here. Hold today's 87% SLA compliance, but don't push agents to shorten calls at the cost of solving the problem.
ChannelTune the chatbot hand-offChatbot CSAT is 0.11 points lower than other channels (p = 0.03). Send negative-sentiment chats to a human agent sooner.
MeasureTrack retention directlyThis data has no repeat-purchase or churn field, so CSAT stands in for retention. Link each contact ID to the customer's orders over the next 90 days, so the effect of service on retention can be measured rather than inferred.
