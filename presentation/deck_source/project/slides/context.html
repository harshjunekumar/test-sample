Business context

Can customer service explain the drop in retention?

01

The problem

Customer retention is falling. Customer service is one of the few places Flipkart talks directly to customers, so it is a likely cause and an easy place to act.

02

The data

30,000 contacts · 13 columns: sentiment, CSAT (1–10), reason, channel, response time vs SLA, call duration, call centre, city/state. No retention or repeat-order field.

03

The approach

Use CSAT as the stand-in for retention. Build a metric tree, test 6 hypotheses in Excel (stats, correlation, pivots), then build an interactive dashboard.

Flipkart customer-service analysis · Oct 2020 data · 2

Retention is not in the dataset, so every conclusion links service to CSAT, and CSAT to retention by assumption. We will come back to this in the recommendations.
