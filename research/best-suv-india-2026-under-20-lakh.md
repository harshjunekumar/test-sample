## 0. Method, sourcing and honest caveats — read this first

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Data collection method | **Live web search (September 2026)** across Nexa/Maruti, Toyota, Hyundai, Kia, Honda, Tata, Skoda, VW, Mahindra listings and CarWale / CarDekho / ZigWheels / Autocar India / 91Wheels / V3Cars / MyCarHelpline / CarTrade / Cars24 / AckoDrive |
| Direct page scraping | **Not possible.** This environment's network egress proxy blocks direct HTTPS fetches to all automotive portals and manufacturer sites. Every figure below therefore comes from live search-engine retrieval of those same pages, not from a rendered page fetch. |
| Ex-showroom prices | Verified against **2 or more independent sources** wherever possible. Where sources disagree, the disagreement is shown explicitly and flagged ⚠. |
| On-road prices | **Computed** from published statutory slabs (see §2), then **cross-validated** against portal on-road quotes. Two independent cross-checks landed within 0.4% — see §2.3. |
| Anything unconfirmed | Marked ⚠ **UNCONFIRMED** and never silently smoothed over. |

**What "on-road" means here.** The on-road figures in §4 are *statutory* on-road: ex-showroom + road tax + registration/HSRP + TCS + FASTag + first-year comprehensive insurance. Dealer quotes in NCR routinely add ₹25,000–₹60,000 more for accessory packages, extended warranty and handling/logistics charges — these are **optional/negotiable** and are deliberately excluded so the comparison stays like-for-like. Insurance is modelled at 3.0% of ex-showroom; actual quotes run ₹45,000–₹75,000 depending on zero-depreciation, engine protect, RSA and NCB.

## 2. Statutory cost structure — the actual reason the three cities differ

### 2.1 Verified tax rates (individual buyer, private registration, petrol)

| Levy | New Delhi | Noida (UP) | Gurugram (Haryana) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One-time road tax** (% of ex-showroom, cars > ₹10L) | **10%** (individual). 12.5% if registered in a company name. | **11%** ⬆ | **8%** (₹6L–₹20L slab) |
| Road tax, other slabs (context) | 4% (< ₹6L), 7% (₹6–10L) | 9% (< ₹10L) | 5% (< ₹6L), 10% (> ₹20L) |
| Registration fee | ₹600 | ₹600 | ₹600 |
| HSRP / number plate | included | ~₹500 | ~₹1,000 |
| **MCD one-time parking fee** | **₹4,000** (ex-showroom > ₹4L) | — | — |
| FASTag (incl. security deposit) | ₹500 | ₹500 | ₹500 |
| **TCS** (Income Tax, 1% on ex-showroom > ₹10L) | 1% | 1% | 1% |
| Hypothecation (only if financed) | ₹1,500 | ₹1,500 | ₹1,500 |
| Insurance (1st-yr comprehensive, modelled) | ~3.0% of ex-showroom | ~3.0% | ~3.0% |

**Important 2026 change — Uttar Pradesh:** the UP Cabinet (chaired by CM Yogi Adityanath) approved a Transport Department proposal amending the UP Motor Vehicle Taxation Act, 1997, raising one-time tax on private light passenger vehicles by **1 percentage point** — from 8% → **9%** below ₹10 lakh and from 10% → **11%** above ₹10 lakh. This is the first hike since 2015 and it makes **Noida the most expensive of the three cities to register in.** Several tax-guide pages still show the old 8%/10% figures; the 9%/11% figures are the current ones and are what this document uses. ⚠ Confirm at your ARTO/dealer on the day of booking.

### 2.2 The formulas (so you can re-run these yourself for any price)

```
New Delhi   on-road = Ex-showroom × 1.14 + ₹5,100
                      (10% road tax + 1% TCS + 3% insurance; +₹4,000 MCD +₹600 reg +₹500 FASTag)

Noida (UP)  on-road = Ex-showroom × 1.15 + ₹1,600
                      (11% road tax + 1% TCS + 3% insurance; +₹600 reg +₹500 HSRP +₹500 FASTag)

Gurugram    on-road = Ex-showroom × 1.12 + ₹2,100
                      (8% road tax + 1% TCS + 3% insurance; +₹600 reg +₹1,000 HSRP +₹500 FASTag)

Add ₹1,500 to any of the above if the car is financed (hypothecation endorsement).
```

## 6. Verdict — which is the "best"?

### 🏆 Overall best: **Maruti Suzuki Grand Vitara Alpha 1.5 AT (NEXA)** — ₹16.55L ex-sh / ₹18.56L–19.05L on-road
Nothing else in the shortlist combines **20.58 kmpl claimed efficiency**, **210 mm ground clearance** (joint-highest here), a panoramic sunroof, wireless charging, 360° camera, head-up display and ventilated seats at this price. The 6-speed torque-converter automatic is the most durable and least fussy AT type in the group. Trade-off: **373 L boot is small** for the segment, and the 1.5 NA engine is relaxed rather than quick.

### 🥈 The mechanical twin: **Toyota Urban Cruiser Hyryder V AT** — ₹17.24L ex-sh
Same platform, same engine, same 210 mm / 373 L / 20.58 kmpl numbers, 20 mm longer. You pay ~₹69,000 more ex-showroom for **Toyota's warranty and service reputation**. If long-term ownership peace-of-mind matters more than the last ₹70k, this is the smarter buy of the two.

### 🥉 Best all-rounder if boot space matters: **Kia Seltos HTX iVT** — ₹15.92L ex-sh
**Cheapest mid-size SUV in the shortlist**, and the new-generation car is now **4,460 mm long with a 2,690 mm wheelbase and 447 L boot** — the biggest cabin here. 12.3-inch screen, panoramic sunroof, wireless charging, ventilated seats, Bose audio, 8-way powered driver's seat. Costs **₹63,000 less ex-showroom than the Grand Vitara** while being a bigger car. Trade-offs: 190 mm ground clearance and 17.7 kmpl.

## 8. Explicit confidence flags — what to re-verify at the dealer

1. ⚠ **Grand Vitara Alpha AT ex-showroom:** sources split between ₹16.55L and ₹16.60L. A separate listing quotes ₹17.16L for "Alpha Smart Hybrid AT" — likely a dual-tone or (O) pack. **Get the on-road quote in writing.**
2. ⚠ **UP road tax 11%:** confirmed via the UP Cabinet amendment reported in 2026 and MyCarHelpline's 2026 slab table, but many tax-guide pages still publish the older 10%. If your ARTO still charges 10%, your Noida figures drop by ~₹17,000 on a ₹17L car.
3. ⚠ **Tata Nexon sunroof type:** listed as both "electric" and "panoramic" across sources. Verify visually.
4. ⚠ **Brezza ZXi+ AT price and boot/GC:** the **2026 Brezza facelift** re-priced and re-specced the range (a turbo-petrol option was added). Figures here should be treated as indicative for the facelift.
5. ⚠ **Maruti Victoris ZXi+ 6AT** and ⚠ **VW Taigun 2026 AT** — deliberately left unpriced rather than estimated.
6. **Insurance** is modelled at a flat 3.0%. Zero-dep + engine protect + RSA will push it to 4–4.5% and add ₹20,000–₹30,000 to every total above.
7. **Dealer extras** (accessory kit, extended warranty, handling) are excluded throughout — expect ₹25,000–₹60,000 more on an actual quotation, and negotiate them.
