Growing monthly revenue from the patient base already on the platform

A patient on metformin, glimepiride and a statin can generate up to 12 refills a year. Across digital pharmacy, seven or eight is more typical. Closing that adherence gap is where the largest share of available upside appears to sit.

Chronic therapy is a recurring annuity

A missed refill month is rarely deferred revenue. The patient buys locally instead, and the ordering habit resets.

One short line can cost the whole basket

The patient needs all three strips together. When one is unavailable, the order is often placed offline in full.

The molecule indicates the test

Metformin implies HbA1c every 90 days. Pharmacy knows the molecule; diagnostics carries roughly twice the margin.
