Case study · Tata 1mg · Growth diagnosis

Tata 1mg doesn't have a traffic problem. It has a refill problem.

A patient on metformin, glimepiride and a statin should generate 12 refills a year. Most platforms capture seven or eight. That gap — not a shortage of traffic — is where the next ₹40 Cr a month lives.

Chronic is an annuity that leaks

A missed refill month isn't deferred revenue. The patient walks to the chemist downstairs and the habit resets.

Out-of-stock is silent churn

One short line splits the basket — the patient needs all three strips together, so the whole order goes offline.

The molecule already names the test

Metformin implies HbA1c every 90 days. Pharmacy knows the molecule; diagnostics carries twice the margin.
